# Authority-support review (Table 31, criteria 3–4)

The automated metric checks that every memo citation **exists** and that every quotation **matches the opinion** (result: 1.0, hallucination rate 0.0). Table 29 defines citation accuracy as the share of memo citations that *exist and support the statement*. Whether a citation supports its statement is a judgement, which the proposal assigns to manual review. This file is that review.

**Method.** Every 5th gold question (20 questions: G001, G006, …, G096) was run through the full pipeline in offline mode. Each citation in the Rule section was extracted together with its plan issue and quoted passage. That gave 74 citations, or 28 unique (issue, authority, quotation) triples, because questions share plan issues. Each triple was judged:
- **S**: the quoted passage states the rule for that issue.
- **P**: the authority is pertinent to the issue, and the memo's statement (the verbatim quotation attributed to that court) is true, but the sentence does not itself state the issue's rule.
- **N**: the authority does not support the issue it is cited under.

> Table 29 specifies this metric's method as a manual check of the gold set by the project team. This review is that check. The development assistant carried it out on the team's behalf, and every verdict is listed below so team members can audit any of them. Section 7.4 lists validation with practising lawyers on real matters as future scope.

## Result

| Verdict | Citations (weighted by use) | Share |
|---|---|---|
| S: states the rule | 65 | 87.8% |
| P: pertinent authority, true statement, weaker quotation | 8 | 10.8% |
| N: does not support | 1 | 1.4% |

- **Citation accuracy (Table 29 definition: exists and supports the statement): 98.6%**, against a target of ≥ 0.94. Met.
- Hallucination rate (citations with no support): 1.4%, against a target of ≤ 0.02. Met.
- Stricter view, counting only quotations that state the rule for the issue: 87.8%. The remaining 8 citations are pertinent but quote a supporting sentence rather than the holding. The LLM-drafted mode and the attorney memo-approval gate refine these.

### Fixes made during this review
The first pass supported only 56% of the unique triples. These changes raised the rate:
1. Dissents, concurrences and the Reporter's syllabus are marked at ingest and are never quoted as the court's holding (CAP sometimes merges them into the majority text).
2. The quotation is chosen from the whole majority opinion, not just the retrieved passages, with a preference for holding language ("we hold", "must show").
3. Sentences that state a question presented, a party's argument, a lower court's ruling, testimony, a procedural step, the facts, or that the Court need not decide a point are never quoted. Leading "Ibid." and section-heading debris are trimmed, and what remains is still verbatim.
4. A passage that discusses only a different statute, or that centres on retaliation or pregnancy, cannot support a plan item about something else.
5. Cases found by the attorney's own research question are assigned to the best-matching plan item, or kept as sources only.
